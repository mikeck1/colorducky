Privacy Policy

Last updated November 21, 2026

ColorDucky doesn't collect personal data. This policy covers the ColorDucky app and this website.

No accounts, ads or tracking

ColorDucky has no sign-in, shows no ads and contains no advertising, tracking or third-party analytics code. We never track you across apps or websites, and we never sell or share data.

Anonymous usage counts

To learn which pages people enjoy and to find crashes, the app keeps simple daily totals on your device, such as "a page was opened 3 times", "a drawing was finished", "the app crashed once", and sends only those totals, once a day, to our own server (hosted by Cloudflare). They include the app version, whether it's an iPad or iPhone, and whether it's a test version of the app, and nothing else: no name, no account, no device or advertising identifier, no location, no drawings. The totals can't be linked to you, and our server doesn't keep IP addresses. You can turn this off any time in the app's Settings ("Share anonymous usage counts"); that also deletes any totals not yet sent.

Your drawings stay on your device

Pages, drawings and settings are stored only on your device (and in your device backups, if you use them). Delete a drawing and it's gone. When you share a drawing or a time-lapse video, it goes only where you choose to send it.

Purchases

ColorDucky Plus is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. The app checks your purchase with the App Store on your device.

Children

ColorDucky is safe for all ages. The only thing it sends is the anonymous daily totals above, which contain no personal information. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Crash reports

If you turn on "Share with App Developers" in your device's settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. They don't identify you.

This website

This site uses no cookies and no analytics.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
