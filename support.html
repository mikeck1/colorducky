Support

Need help with ColorDucky? Email colorducky@icloud.com and we'll get back to you.

Restore ColorDucky Plus

On a new device, open ColorDucky, go to Settings and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought Plus with. Family members get Plus through Family Sharing automatically.

Cancel or change a subscription

Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions ▸ ColorDucky. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period. Your drawings stay yours after Plus ends.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. To ask for a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where are my drawings?

Drawings are saved on your device only. They're included in your device's iCloud or computer backup, so restoring a backup brings them back. Deleting the app deletes its drawings.

Share a time-lapse video

In any drawing, tap ••• ▸ Time-lapse video, then choose where to send it from the share sheet.
